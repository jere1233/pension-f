# AutoNest Privacy Policy

## Last Updated: April 6, 2026

### Introduction
AutoNest is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile banking application.

### Information We Collect

#### SMS Permissions
AutoNest requires SMS permissions to automatically process M-Pesa transactions. We only read SMS messages from M-Pesa (identified by sender "MPESA" or "M-PESA").

**What we access:**
- M-Pesa transaction SMS messages only
- Transaction amounts and recipient phone numbers
- We do NOT read personal messages, emails, or other SMS content

**Why we need this:**
- To automatically detect successful M-Pesa transactions
- To trigger deposit confirmations without manual input
- To provide seamless banking experience

#### Data Storage
- User account information (stored securely)
- Transaction history (encrypted)
- App preferences

### Data Security
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- SMS content is processed locally and not stored permanently
- We follow industry-standard security practices

### Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:
- As required for M-Pesa transaction processing
- With your explicit consent
- As required by law

### Your Rights
- You can revoke SMS permissions at any time in app settings
- You can request deletion of your account and data
- You can opt out of automated processing

### Contact Us
For privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@autonest.com

### Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally. Users will be notified of significant changes.
